What happened on this trip

Our taxi

    What we changed

    It picks you up and drops you off

    The original car only drives in circles. Ours pulls up to the curb, waits while you get in, and stops again where you're going.

    It brakes gently

    Instead of stopping suddenly, it starts slowing early and evenly, and stops right at the line instead of far before it.

    It tells you why

    The screen says what the car is doing, what it sees and where it turns next, so nothing comes as a surprise. It also only stops where it should: not for yield signs or signs meant for another road.

    How it works (technical details)
    Taken from the files we were given
    • Route: the outer lap (path 4 in SDCS_Paths_7.mat) from the taxi hub.
    • Every sign, stop line, crosswalk and traffic light from Setup_Real_Scenario.m.
    • Speed logic from the Simulink model: 0.5 m/s, slowing in turns by cos(steering)¹⁰, 3 m/s² limit, 6 s start-up.
    • Sign detection: red pixels (hue 0 ± 20°, saturation and brightness ≥ 100), blob ≥ 100 px, closer than 1.2 m, then a 2 s stop.
    What our code adds
    • taxi_controller.m: pickup, drop-off, gentle braking, stop at the line.
    • classify_red_sign.m: octagon vs triangle from how much of its box the red blob fills, and narrow blobs mean the sign faces another road.
    • rider_screen/: this passenger screen in MATLAB.
    Assumed until measured in QLabs
    • Pickup at (1.25, −1.09), drop-off at the taxi hub.
    • Sign size 0.075 m (stop) and 0.09 m (yield); camera 0.2 m ahead of the car's centre.
    • Ideal blob fill (83 % octagon, 50 % triangle); calibrate with calibrate_sign_shapes.m.
    • The car stays on its path; distances on the screen are shown at real-world size (the model car is 1:10).
